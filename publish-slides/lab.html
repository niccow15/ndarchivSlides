MODEL & CONTROLS

Actual agents. Actual random samples.

Every round, all agents independently sample uniformly from all n agents, with replacement and self-sampling. They read the configuration at the start of the round and update simultaneously.

3-Majority: sample three agents, adopt the majority opinion, and choose uniformly among the three opinions when all differ.

2-Choices: sample two agents. Adopt their opinion if they agree; otherwise retain the current opinion.

The seeded pseudorandom generator makes Reset reproduce the same run. The two comparison panels use separate random streams. A panel freezes when it reaches consensus; the other continues. One run illustrates behavior, not an asymptotic bound.

Bars are ranked by current size. Colors stay attached to opinion identities. Every singleton has its own identity, even when shades look similar. The dashed histogram line is the initial maximum; its value stays fixed. Both comparison histograms share a vertical scale, which expands together as needed.

In the proof view, “comparable densities” means m < 2γ, the threshold used for this demonstration. It is an operational threshold for the broader asymptotic notion of balance.

Keyboard: Space runs or pauses; Right Arrow advances a round; R resets; F toggles fullscreen; 1, 2, 3 switch views. In One round, Space alternates sampling and applying the round.

Changing the protocol, population, or seed resets the simulations. Switching views pauses playback and preserves the comparison trajectory. The One round view has its own trajectory.
